=== THE CAMERA (keep it exactly as in ref-game.png middle and right) ===
- Pointy-top HEXAGONS (a pointed corner at the top and bottom of each hex, straight vertical sides left and right).
- A SLIGHT TILT: the board is seen from a little south of overhead. Every hex is squashed vertically to about 72% of its width-proportional height. NO vanishing point: a far hex is exactly as big as a near one, rows stay perfectly straight. This is NOT a 2:1 isometric diamond view.
- Each hex is a THICK TILE, like a piece of a board game: under its two lower edges a short side face (about 1/6 of the hex radius deep) of packed brown earth with a hint of roots and stone, the right face in shade. The row in front hides that side, so it only shows along the near (bottom) rim of the board, and where explored ground meets the mist.
- Only the ground is tilted. Castles, trees, mountains, buildings and figures stand UPRIGHT on their hex like miniatures, seen from the same three-quarter angle as the city's models in ref-game.png left.

=== WHAT A HEX LOOKS LIKE ===
- Its ground is a terrain plate painted in the city's style: lush grass with soft colour variation and tiny flowers (grassland), golden-green meadow (plains), warm sand with ripples (desert), grey rocky ground (mountain hex, with the mountain model on it).
- 0 to 3 objects on a hex: a forest (a cluster of the forest trees from ref-assets.png), a mountain, golden fertile fields, a pair of wild boars (game), a building. Generous empty ground around them; never more than three things.
- Hex edges: a thin, soft, slightly darker seam where two hexes meet — no black grid lines.
- OWNERSHIP is a coloured BORDER along the outer edge of a player's territory (a crisp line about 6 px with a soft inner glow), never a tint over the ground. Ours is ROYAL BLUE. Rivals: crimson, emerald, amber, violet, teal.
- FOG: Revealed = full colour. Sensed = the same hex darker, desaturated and veiled by thin drifting mist, objects as silhouettes. Unknown = soft, puffy white-grey clouds sitting on the tile; never a flat grey void, never black.
- Beyond the board's edge: an endless soft sea of clouds, pale blue-grey, a little lighter at the top.
- The centre hex is the DARK PORTAL: a ring of dark standing stones on cracked dark stone, a deep violet swirling void inside.
