# Offers — the prompts (2026-10-06)

Mockups of the monetization offers (M86–M94), one portrait phone screen
each. Layout taken from Kingshot's store (a tab strip of offers over one big
offer card: title, pitch, countdown, big art, a gem sack panel with item
tiles, a starburst value badge, a purchase-limit line and a price button);
costume taken from ours — wood, parchment, brass, wax, rope.

Attached to every prompt (`m86-refs/`):

- `ref-game-screens.png` — the game today (map, Bag, Settings) at 390×844.
- `ref-ui-kit.png` — the shipped UI pieces, labelled.
- `ref-offer-icons.png` — the currency, key, speed-up, chest, flask, boost,
  builder, compass and Townhall icons, labelled.
- `ref-heroes.png` — six heroes in the cartoon style (Elven Princess,
  Golden Dragon, Scout, Adventurer, Warrior, Merchant).
- `ref-store-m23.png`, `ref-store-m5.png` — earlier store mockups in our style.

After M87 came back and the style was locked, `m87-offer-novice-chain.png`
was attached to every later prompt as the layout anchor (in place of
`ref-store-m5.png`, to stay under the 10 MB upload), and this paragraph went
in after the opening line:

> STYLE LOCKED: the attached m87-offer-novice-chain.png is the approved screen of this same Offers menu. Match it exactly — the same coin plank, header beam, tab strip (same tabs, same look), parchment card in its carved frame, wooden tray, gem sack, brass-rimmed item tiles, red wax value seal, gold price button, lettering and illustration style. Only the content described below changes.

## Shared block

GENERATE A NEW IMAGE. Do not edit or export the attached files: they are ONLY style and layout references.

This is a mockup for a cozy medieval city-builder for phones: one portrait phone screen at 390×844 proportions, no device bezel, no hands, no captions outside the screen. Build it from the game's own pieces in the attached references — the coin plank at the top, the wooden window with the red close knob, parchment panels, painted wood/green/gold/purple buttons, wax seals, rope, brass — so it looks like the same game as ref-game-screens and the earlier store mockups. Warm natural materials lit from above: wood, yellowed parchment, brass, wax, cloth; never flat fills, never blue plastic gloss. A symbol on a piece is carved or embossed into it. Use the icons from ref-offer-icons exactly as drawn there (Gems are the blue-violet crystal; Mana is the blue-violet swirl orb; gold and silver keys; the winged hourglass-and-hammer construction speed-ups with a red time ribbon; the "?" choice chests); do not redesign them. Big illustrations follow the heroes' flat cartoon style in ref-heroes: bold uniform dark-brown outline, flat colour, one shadow tone, one highlight. All text in English, short and legible, spelled exactly as written here. Prices in US dollars.

THE OFFERS SCREEN LAYOUT (every offer, except where the screen says otherwise): the coin plank at the very top (Gold 12,400 · Food 860 · Wood 3,120 · Stone 940 · Mana 64 · Gems 500). Under it a wooden header beam with a back arrow knob and the title "Offers". Under it a horizontal strip of TABS, one per offer, like wooden file tabs with a parchment face: each tab a small icon of that offer and a short name under it; the selected tab is lighter and joins the card below; a small red wax dot on the corner of a tab that is new; the strip runs off the right edge. Tabs, left to right: "First buy" (hero portrait), "Novice" (chest), "Explorer" (compass), "Hero slot" (helmet), "Mana" (flask), "Build" (hammer speed-up), "Daily" (wax-sealed pack). Below, one big offer CARD fills the screen: a parchment card in a carved wooden frame. Inside: the offer's title in large dark-brown serif lettering, a one-line pitch under it, a small dark wooden chip with a brass clock and a countdown where the offer has one; then a big cartoon illustration on a warm sunburst of light; then a recessed wooden tray holding a cloth sack spilling Gems with the Gem count big beside it, and under that a row of square item tiles — each a small parchment tile in a brass rim with the icon, a count in its bottom-right corner and, where it has one, a size label at the top ("1h", "5m", "10K"); pinned over the tray's top-right corner a big red WAX SEAL with a wavy edge stamped with the value, like "1,200%" (our version of the starburst value badge). Under the tray a small muted line for the purchase limit, and a big gold painted button with the price. The bottom nav bar is hidden (a menu is open).
