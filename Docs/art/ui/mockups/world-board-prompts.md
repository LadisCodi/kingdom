=== WHAT THE WORLD BOARD IS ===
- A shared board of pointy-top HEXAGONS (a pointed corner at the top and bottom of each hex, flat sides left and right), six players on one board.
- Camera: looking almost straight down at the board — it is a map you read and count, not a diorama. The hexes themselves are flat, top-down terrain plates that tile seamlessly; the 3D objects standing on them (castles, trees, mountains, camps) are the same models as ref-assets.png seen from a high three-quarter angle, so they stand up off the hex like miniatures on a board.
- A hex shows its terrain (grassland, plains, desert, mountain ground) plus 0 to 3 objects: a forest, a mountain, fertile fields, wild game, a building. Never more than three or four things on one hex; generous empty grass around them.
- Hex edges are visible as a thin, soft, slightly darker seam on revealed ground — no black grid lines.
- OWNERSHIP is a coloured BORDER running along the outer edge of a player's territory (a crisp line about 6 px with a soft inner glow), never a tint over the ground. Our kingdom is ROYAL BLUE. Rivals: crimson red, emerald green, amber gold, violet, teal.
- A claimable hex (neutral, next to our territory) has a warm golden rim on its edge, never a fill.
- FOG has three states:
  - Revealed: full colour.
  - Sensed: the same terrain and objects but darker, desaturated and half-veiled by thin drifting mist; objects show as silhouettes.
  - Unknown: opaque, soft, rolling white-grey mist clouds; the hex is not there at all. Never a flat grey void, never black.
- The centre hex of the board is the DARK PORTAL: an ancient ring of dark stone set in cracked ground, with a deep violet swirling void inside (dim when shut).
- Our city hex holds our castle (the Townhall from ref-assets.png, with blue banners) inside a low stone wall, a few houses around it.
