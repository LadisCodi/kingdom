WORLD HEX SPRITES — TEST SHEET 1 (two sprites) for the game "Kingdom".

GENERATE A NEW IMAGE. Do not edit or export the attached files: they are ONLY style references.
- ref-board.png = the world board we are building: tilted pointy-top hex tiles, bright stylized-3D, lit from above by a midday sun. Match its trees, rocks, fields and animals exactly in render, saturation and scale.
- ref-assets.png = sprites that already ship in the game. Same render, same chunky rounded silhouettes, same saturation. Your sprites will sit next to these.

WHAT THESE ARE
Each sprite is the CONTENT of one hex of the world board, standing on a separate ground tile the game draws under it. So: NO ground, NO grass, NO soil, NO base, NO platform, NO hex outline, NO drop shadow. Only the objects, on full transparency.
- The camera is the one in ref-board.png: from a little south of overhead, objects seen from a high three-quarter angle, standing upright.
- Each sprite must fit on a hex: imagine a hex tile 1000 px wide seen at this tilt — its top face is an elongated hexagon 1000 px wide and about 720 px tall. Every object of the sprite stands INSIDE that face; the leftmost and rightmost points of the drawing are no wider than 900 px. Things may rise above it.
- Draw as if the sprite will be shown about 130 px wide on a phone: big readable shapes, no tiny details.

THE SHEET: one landscape image, 1536x1024, two sprites side by side, LEFT and RIGHT halves. Nothing may cross the vertical midline: keep a clear 60 px band of transparency down the middle.

LEFT — "Forested mountain": one big grey-rock mountain (like the mountains in ref-board.png and ref-assets.png, two or three peaks, a little moss), its lower slopes and foot wrapped by a band of the same round deciduous trees as ref-board.png's forests — trees on the left, right and front of the mountain base, a few climbing the slope. It reads at a glance as ONE place: a wooded mountain. About 70% of the width is mountain.

RIGHT — "Fertile land with game": two or three rectangular golden wheat fields with soft furrows laid flat as if on the ground (they are the only thing here that lies flat), a short wooden fence along one of them, a couple of hay stooks, and in front a pair of wild boars (like ref-board.png's boars) — the boars a little to the front-left of the fields. No farmhouse, no buildings.

Do not draw grid lines, cell borders, labels, captions, shadows or any background. The background must be alpha 0 everywhere, not white, not a checkerboard. Then apply the true-alpha transparency correction and give me the download link for the corrected PNG.
