=== SHARED PREAMBLE (every sheet) ===
GENERATE NEW IMAGES. Do not edit or export the attached files: they are ONLY style references.
- ref-hexes.png = hex sprites that already ship in the game. THE STYLE IS LOCKED ON THEM: same render, same saturation, same outline softness, same light from above, same scale of trees, rocks, houses, same camera. Your sprites sit next to these on the same board: match them exactly.
- ref-precious.png = the three precious materials. Each deposit must make its material instantly recognisable: HEARTWOOD = logs whose cut ends glow warm amber-orange with a spiral; STARMETAL = a silver-blue star-ore with a glowing white star at its heart; MOONGLASS = pale lavender-blue crystals with a soft glow.

WHAT A SPRITE IS
- The CONTENT of one hex of the world board, standing on a separate ground tile the game draws under it. NO ground plate, NO base, NO hex outline, NO drop shadow. Only the objects, on full transparency. Small tufts of grass, bushes and short dirt paths between objects are fine, as in ref-hexes.png.
- Camera: from a little south of overhead, objects seen from a high three-quarter angle, standing upright.
- Footprint: a hex tile seen at this tilt has a top face like an elongated hexagon, about 1.4 times wider than tall. Every object stands inside it; things may rise above it.
- Draw as if shown about 130 px wide on a phone: big readable shapes, no tiny details.
- Districts INCLUDE the deposit they work, as the logging camp includes its forest. Blue roofs, cream walls and stone, golden timber, as in ref-hexes.png.

EACH SHEET: one landscape image, 1536x1024, FOUR sprites in a 2x2 grid (top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right). Nothing may cross the vertical or the horizontal midline: keep a clear 60 px band of transparency along both midlines. Every sprite about the same size relative to its quadrant as in ref-hexes.png.

End of every request: Do not draw grid lines, cell borders, labels, captions, shadows or any background. The background must be alpha 0 everywhere, not white, not a checkerboard. Then apply the true-alpha transparency correction and give me the download link for the corrected PNG. Do not re-render to fix a prop count.
