# Spanish glossary

Spain Spanish. The UI says **tú** (imperatives: *Construye*, *Mejora*, *Toca*).
The cast calls the player **Majestad** and says **usted** (*Toque la niebla,
Majestad*). Buttons carry one verb, as in English: **Construir**, **Mejorar**,
**Entrenar**, **Acelerar**, **Mover**, **Recoger**, **Reclamar**, **Cerrar**.
Title case is English only: Spanish capitalizes the first word and proper
names (*Mejorar a nivel 2*, not *Mejorar A Nivel 2*).

Keep proper names of people as they are (Isolde, Hob, Kofi, Priya, Marisol,
Idris, Bess, Wren, Grukk, Tom Miller) — but translate *Old Hob* → *el viejo
Hob*, *Master Kofi* → *maestro Kofi*. Oakville stays **Oakville**.

## Time

`s`, `m`, `h`, `d` stay as they are (*5m 3s*, *2d 4h*). *instant* → *al
instante*. *Ready* → *Listo*. *Full* → *Lleno*. *Free* → *Gratis*.
